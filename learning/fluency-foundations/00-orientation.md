# Orientation — Fluency Foundations (Tier 0)

## What this is

This is the earliest tier in the five-tier path — a companion set for two
Anthropic Academy courses that calibrate *how to think* about working with
Claude before you touch any specific feature or product surface:

- **AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations** —
  https://anthropic.skilljar.com/ai-fluency-framework-foundations
- **AI Capabilities and Limitations** —
  https://anthropic.skilljar.com/ai-capabilities-and-limitations

**Honesty note, same as every tier:** these documents are built from
Anthropic's public materials (including the publicly-available AI Fluency
Framework PDF and aifluencyframework.org, the framework's own public site)
rather than the gated Skilljar lesson content itself. Register for both
courses for free at the links above.

## Why start here, even though it's not Claude-Code-specific

Everything downstream — Claude 101, Claude Code, Skills, Subagents, MCP — is
still, underneath, a human deciding when and how to work with an AI system.
The Fluency Framework gives you shared vocabulary for that decision (the
"4 Ds"), and AI Capabilities and Limitations grounds your expectations in
how these models actually work, rather than either science-fiction hype or
dismissiveness. Fifteen minutes here changes how you read every later tier.

## What you'll be able to do after finishing this tier

- Name the 4 Ds and use them to reason about a real delegation decision, not
  just recite them.
- Explain, without hand-waving, at least two concrete ways an AI model like
  Claude can fail or mislead — so you know what to check before trusting an
  output.
